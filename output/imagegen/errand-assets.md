# Everyday errand art

Generated with the built-in ChatGPT Image tool. Four separate background prompts; interactive objects are rendered by the game for accurate hit targets and animation.

Shared direction: stylized-concept, square production background for a polished casual mobile game. Hand painted Vietnamese Saigon street atmosphere, warm amber and deep teal colors, readable shapes, subtle textures and soft shadows. Keep the central 75–80% uncluttered for interactive overlays. No text, characters, hands, UI, logos or watermark.
